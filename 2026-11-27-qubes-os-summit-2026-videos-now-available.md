Recordings of the presentations from [Qubes OS Summit 2026](https://pretix.eu/qubes/summit2026/) are now available here:

<https://media.ccc.de/c/qubesos26>

**Note:** Presenters had the option to request that their presentations not be recorded. If a presenter opted out of recording, there was a "no recording" icon next to that presentation in the [conference schedule](https://pretalx.com/qubes-os-summit-2026/schedule/). Only on-site attendees were able to view those presentations. They were not livestreamed or recorded.

Thank you to our sponsors, presenters, and attendees for making Qubes OS Summit 2026 a success!

## Gold-tier sponsor

[![Freedom of the Press Foundation logo](/attachment/site/fpf-logo.svg)](https://freedom.press/)

[Freedom of the Press Foundation (FPF)](https://freedom.press/) is a long-standing [Qubes Partner](https://www.qubes-os.org/partners/). As a nonprofit organization, it is dedicated to supporting and defending public interest journalism. It leads the development of [SecureDrop](https://securedrop.org/), an open-source whistleblower submission platform used by more than 50 media organizations around the world to securely accept documents from anonymous sources. FPF uses Qubes OS in the development of an integrated [SecureDrop Workstation](https://github.com/freedomofpress/securedrop-workstation).

## Silver-tier sponsors

[![NovaCustom logo](/attachment/site/novacustom-logo.png)](https://novacustom.com/)

[NovaCustom](https://novacustom.com/) builds custom laptops, mini PCs, and smartphones with a focus on privacy, security, and customization. They offer the freedom of Dasharo coreboot firmware, true repairability, and a plethora of customization options. Several NovaCustom models are [officially certified for Qubes OS](https://doc.qubes-os.org/en/latest/user/hardware/certified-hardware/certified-hardware.html).

[![Nitrokey logo](/attachment/site/nitrokey-logo.svg)](https://www.nitrokey.com/)

[Nitrokey](https://www.nitrokey.com/) is a world leader in open-source security hardware. They develop hardware for data encryption, key management, user authentication, network devices, desktops, laptops, and smartphones. Several NitroPads and NitroPCs are [officially certified for Qubes OS](https://doc.qubes-os.org/en/latest/user/hardware/certified-hardware/certified-hardware.html).
